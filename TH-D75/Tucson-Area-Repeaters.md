Channels programmed in [`Tucson-Area_TH-D75.THD75`](Tucson-Area_TH-D75.THD75). Open that file in the Kenwood TH-D75 programming software and write it to the radio.

Frequencies, tones and modes were checked against the Radio Society of Tucson repeater chart (the Repeaters page on the RST website) on 2026-10-21. "Output" is what the radio receives; "Input" is what it transmits on.

## Notes

- **6 m channels (035–037) are receive-only.** The TH-D75 can listen on 6 m but can't transmit there.
- **D-STAR (003, 025)** needs a D-STAR registration (per the RST chart). Channel 003 is routed `CQCQCQ` via `K7TUS  B` / `K7TUS  G`; 025 has no routing callsigns set yet.
- **PECOC (005)** uses an odd 300 kHz split (input 147.600), as listed on the RST chart.
- **TUMAMOC (006)** uses an odd 1.02 MHz split.
- **GRN VLY (014) and RIO RICO (015)** are probably out of range from Tucson. They're still in the scan list.
- **LEMN ZZY (022)** uses DCS 023 instead of a CTCSS tone and is linked to 146.940.

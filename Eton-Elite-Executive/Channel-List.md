# Channel list — Eton Elite Executive

Suggested memories for the Eton Elite Executive, a receive-only AM/FM/LW/shortwave/airband portable with SSB. It has no computer programming interface, so enter these by hand. A reviewer counted about 700 memories; the F1–F7 keys look like memory pages, so the groups below are laid out one per page.

The radio covers FM 87.5–108 MHz, AM 520–1710 kHz, longwave 150–285 kHz, shortwave 1.711–30 MHz and airband 118–136 MHz. It doesn't reach NOAA weather (162 MHz), 2 m/70 cm ham or military UHF aviation, so those stay on the TH-D75.

## AM broadcast and shortwave broadcasters

Not listed. Use the radio's auto-tuning to store Tucson AM stations, and tune the shortwave broadcast bands rather than storing stations, because shortwave schedules change every season. The main bands are 49 m (5.9–6.2 MHz), 41 m (7.2–7.45), 31 m (9.4–9.9), 25 m (11.6–12.1), 22 m (13.57–13.87), 19 m (15.1–15.8), 16 m (17.48–17.9) and 13 m (21.45–21.85). Lower bands work best at night, higher bands in daytime.
